# Cold Mail Agent

An AI-powered tool that helps job seekers identify relevant recruiters and generate personalized cold emails for job opportunities.

## Why I Created This

Finding the right person to contact for a job application is often as difficult as finding the job itself. Job seekers may spend significant time searching for recruiters, determining who is relevant to a specific role, finding contact information, and writing personalized outreach.

I created Cold Mail Agent to reduce that manual effort while keeping the candidate in control of the final outreach.

## What It Does

The user provides a **job description and resume**, and the system:

1. Analyzes the job requirements and candidate's background.
2. Finds potentially relevant recruiters associated with the company.
3. Ranks recruiters based on their relevance to the opportunity.
4. Finds available professional contact information.
5. Identifies evidence connecting the recruiter, company, and role.
6. Generates a personalized cold email based on that evidence.
7. Keeps the user in control before the email is sent.

The goal is not simply to generate an email, but to help the user decide **who to contact and why**, then turn that decision into relevant outreach.

## Who Benefits

### Job Seekers

* Spend less time searching for the right recruiter.
* Avoid sending generic outreach to random contacts.
* Get personalized emails based on their actual experience.
* Make the outreach process more systematic.

### Students & Early-Career Candidates

The project is particularly useful for candidates who may not have an established professional network and need a structured way to identify relevant people within a company.

### Career Services & Recruiting Communities

The workflow can also serve as a foundation for tools that help candidates improve networking and job-search efficiency at scale.

## Product Thinking

A key design principle of Cold Mail Agent is:

> **Relevance over volume. Evidence over assumptions. Human control over automation.**

Rather than automatically contacting as many people as possible, the product focuses on helping the user find a **small number of relevant contacts** and make better-informed outreach decisions.

## Current Status

Cold Mail Agent is an evolving project. The initial MVP focuses on recruiter discovery, relevance ranking, contact discovery, evidence matching, and personalized email generation.

Future iterations will focus on stronger evaluation, product analytics, user feedback, outreach tracking, and integrations with email platforms.
